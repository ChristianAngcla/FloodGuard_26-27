# LIKELY PANEL QUESTIONS & AUTHORITATIVE DEFENSE ANSWERS

### Q1: What are the warning thresholds for Tumana Bridge?
**Answer:** Tumana Bridge has operational PAGASA water-level thresholds of 17.26 EL.m Alert, 18.26 EL.m Alarm, and 19.26 EL.m Critical. Because the Tumana model output uses the compatible station EL.m basis, FloodGuard may classify the daily prediction into a forecast threshold band. This forecast classification remains separate from the actual live emergency state, which is determined only by observed telemetry.

### Q2: Does a high daily forecast trigger emergency sirens or evacuations?
**Answer:** No. FloodGuard strictly enforces a Two-Path Architecture. Daily forecasts provide decision-support planning advisories for LGU disaster managers. Live emergency notifications and evacuation protocols are triggered exclusively by actual live telemetry crossing official thresholds.

### Q3: Why is Multiple Linear Regression (MLR) used instead of Black-Box Deep Learning?
**Answer:** MLR provides complete transparency, reproducible closed-form mathematical coefficients, verifiable HC3 robust standard errors, collinearity guarantees (VIF < 2.1), and deterministic execution suitable for life-critical disaster management.
